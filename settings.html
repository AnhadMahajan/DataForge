DataForge v1.0.0 (Evaluation Build)

Client-Side Deterministic Synthetic Data Intelligence Engine

SMOTE (Chawla et al.)
Calculates Euclidean nearest neighbors on Min-Max normalized feature spaces and interpolates synthetic samples along convex feature line segments.
Gaussian Jittering
Generates zero-mean perturbations scaled by per-feature standard deviations ($\sigma \cdot 0.1$) via Box-Muller transform to avoid exact sample duplication.
Controlled Evaluation
Executes stratified train/test partitioning prior to augmentation. Test sets are strictly held out to guarantee 0% data leakage across all trials.
Degradation Detection
Monitors per-class precision and recall deltas. Flags failure if majority precision drops $>3.5\%$ and issues honest caution warnings.
